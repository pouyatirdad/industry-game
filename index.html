      You trade only with nations you hold a pact with. Click one to see its terms and sign — or wait, and one may come to you.

      Commodity Made Used Sold Out In Held

      What am I reading? Every commodity your nation touched. Made and Used are your own factories; Sold is what your people bought over the counter. In counts everything bought abroad, and the figure in brackets is the part that went to your factories rather than your people — a country with no coalfield can still run a steel mill this way. Held is warehouse stock right now.
      How the score is worked out Every nation is measured against the best in the world on six counts and the score is the weighted total out of 100: the size of its economy (30), the industry it has built (20), what that industry turns out per tick (20), how well its own people are supplied (15), its treasury (10) and its trade balance (5).
